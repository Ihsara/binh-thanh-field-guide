A district of creeks

Before the city reached it, this was villages, trees and open ground, cut through by creeks.

This French survey of 1895 draws this ground before the city's streets reached it. North of the Thị Nghè creek, creeks branch through the land between Bình Hòa and the river, with villages and patches of trees between them. Its village names Bình Lợi Trung and Bình Quới Tây echo in two of today's wards, Bình Lợi Trung and Bình Quới.

1895the year a French survey drew this ground as villages, trees and branching creeks

It cannot be laid on today's map. With the fit it was given before, its check points land a median 1,218 m from where they really are, far too loose to put under today's streets. So it hangs here as a picture, not an overlay: read it for what was there, not for exactly where.

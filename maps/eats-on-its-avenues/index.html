Online, Bình Thạnh eats on its avenues

Most of its buildings stand back in the hẻm; most of its listed kitchens face the avenues.

Online, Bình Thạnh eats on its avenues: per building, an avenue address is five to eight times as likely to hold a listed kitchen as a home in the hẻm, in every ward. One map shows where the district's 44,723 buildings stand, the other where Overture lists its kitchens.

~7×as likely, per building, that an avenue address holds a listed kitchen as a home in the hẻm

Only 26.3% of the buildings stand on an avenue. Of the listed kitchens, 71% do. Ward by ward, the gap runs between 5.3× and 7.8×.

To be on this map a kitchen needs a page online: 99% of the listed ones have a social-media page. A stall that sells to its own hẻm may never need one.

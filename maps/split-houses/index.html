About 1 in 6 numbered listings in Bình Thạnh carry a split number: 3,793 of 24,349. A split number has a letter or "bis" in the house or alley number, like 4A or 12 bis.

Each main street on the map is painted by its own share. From its west end, the part of its length that matches the share of its listings with a split number is in the first colour; the rest is grey. The paint shows a share, not where along the street the split doors are.

On the three streets that old Ward 25's listings also write D1, D2 or D3, the share is 8%; on the district's other main streets it is 16%. How we know says why that gap stays a note and is not this page's claim.

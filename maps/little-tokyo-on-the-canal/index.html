Little Tokyo on Phạm Viết Chánh

By Cầu Thị Nghè 2, Japanese kitchens crowd a few blocks as they do nowhere else in Bình Thạnh.

On Phạm Viết Chánh and Mê Linh, a short walk from the Thị Nghè canal, the signs turn Japanese. Izakaya, yakitori grills and sushi counters sit a few doors apart: in the small circle of streets this map enlarges, Overture lists 45 places it labels Japanese. Their streets are red here, and the thicker the line, the more of them on that stretch.

11×as common here as across the district: 45 of Bình Thạnh's 127 Japanese places sit in this one small circle of streets

They have company. On those blocks the map also lists 8 Western kitchens, in teal: taco bars, a burger grill, French restaurants. A craft beer taproom and a run of small bars sit among them.

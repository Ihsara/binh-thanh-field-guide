Little Seoul between two campuses

A small circle of streets near UTH and HUTECH, where kimbap and mì cay turn up in the names of places listed as Korean.

On Nguyễn Gia Trí and Võ Oanh, between two campuses, the listings turn Korean. In the small circle of streets this map enlarges, 183 places are listed as food or drink, and 9 of them as Korean. Their stretches are red; every other listing's is grey. One stands alone: Cheese Pasta, the circle's only place filed first as Italian. Each word on the red turns up in the listings of more than one of them: kimbap, mì cay.

6.6×as common here as across the district: 9 of the district's 41 places listed as Korean sit in this one small circle of streets

UTH (Trường ĐH Giao thông vận tải) is across the road. HUTECH, which has a Korean studies faculty and a Vietnam-Korea institute, is 8 minutes' walk away.

Not every listing holds up: near HUTECH, just outside this circle, Overture also files its Korean studies faculty and a phone shop as Korean restaurants. We took those out of every count here.

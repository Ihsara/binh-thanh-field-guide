Before the towers

Before the tower that became the country's tallest, this riverbank was a military port.

Bình Thạnh's tallest tower stands on the old Newport base. Work on the port, Tân Cảng, began in 1965: deep-water berths on the Saigon River and a logistics base for the American and South Vietnamese armies.

1965the year work began on Newport, a supply base for the American and South Vietnamese armies

On the 1984 sheet the ground under today's tower is drawn as long buildings between looping roads, with docks marked along the bank. The filled shape is the tower's footprint today. The sheet is registered to its own drawn roads and bridges, which sit a median 22.6 m from where they really are.

Later the apartment blocks of Vinhomes Central Park went up on the port site, and the tower rose above them: 461.2 m tall, finished in 2018, and the tallest building in the country when it opened.

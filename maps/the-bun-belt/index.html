The Bún Belt

Go ward by ward, and in almost every one, the dish on the most signs is bún.

Phở gets the fame; bún gets the streets. We took the food places in each of Bình Thạnh's 5 new wards and set aside cafés and bakeries. We also left out styles rather than dishes (seafood, BBQ, fast food), the catch-all “Vietnamese (other)” and eateries that name no dish. Then we asked which dish comes first. In 4 of the wards it is bún: rice vermicelli, under grilled pork, in crab broth, or with a mound of herbs.

4of the 5 wards have bún as their leading dish

The odd one out is Bình Lợi Trung, where Cơm, the rice plates, comes first. Across the whole district, 219 places are classified as bún shops.

Is this what people eat most, or only what kitchens put on the sign? A classification can tell you what is offered, not what is ordered. → Little Tokyo on the Canal, p. 04

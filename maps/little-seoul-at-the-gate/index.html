Little Seoul at HUTECH's gate

A few steps from a university gate, the menus switch to mì cay and gimbap.

On Nguyễn Gia Trí, by the gate of HUTECH, the signs turn Korean. In the small circle of streets this map enlarges, Overture lists 11 places it labels Korean: bowls of mì cay, the spicy noodles, rolls of gimbap, jjamppong seafood soup, Korean fast food. Their streets are red here, and the thicker the line, the more of them on that stretch. The outline is the campus, 93 m from the pocket's centre.

5.7×as common here as across the district: 11 of Bình Thạnh's 46 Korean places sit in this one small circle of streets

Places labelled Korean are rare across the district, which is why so many in so small a circle stand out. Not every label holds up: the list includes the university's own Korean studies department, filed as a restaurant.

Bình Thạnh eats in pockets

Its food is not spread evenly: one kind of kitchen keeps crowding into a few streets.

Bình Thạnh does not eat evenly; it eats in pockets. We moved a small circle across the map, keeping each spot where one kind of kitchen is at least twice as common as across the district. Each pocket's streets take its colour, and the thicker the line, the more kitchens on that stretch.

7pockets on this map: a few streets each, where one kind of kitchen is at least twice as common as across the district

Most sit next to something you can find on a map. Little Tokyo lies by the Thị Nghè canal, near the Cầu Thị Nghè 2 bridge into the city centre. Little Seoul sits at the gate of HUTECH, a university, and the café street on Đặng Thùy Trâm by Văn Lang University. One seafood and nhậu pocket wraps the Hàng Xanh roundabout on Xô Viết Nghệ Tĩnh; the other sits just off the Phạm Văn Đồng boulevard. Both quán chay pockets hide in the hẻm off Phan Đăng Lưu and off Lê Quang Định; what draws them there, the map does not say.

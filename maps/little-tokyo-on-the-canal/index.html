Little Tokyo on the Canal

Where Bình Thạnh meets the canal, its Japanese kitchens crowd into a few blocks.

44.9%of the district's 127 Japanese places sit inside this one small corner

One small corner holds a large share of the district's Japanese kitchens. Across the Thị Nghè canal lies Đa Kao, a quarter of the city centre. On the Bình Thạnh bank facing it, inside the box this map enlarges, Overture lists 57 places it labels Japanese, sushi or ramen restaurants. The same box holds 111 cafés.

Follow the red dots and they gather along a handful of streets, with the grey cafés scattered around and between them.

Who eats here? The data can say where the restaurants are, not who sits at the counter. That makes it a question worth walking over to ask. → Never Far from a Chain, p. 05

Twenty Wards, Then Five

Until 2025 the district had 20 numbered wards; the same ground is now 5 named ones.

20numbered wards, redrawn as 5 named wards in 2025

Before 2025, an address in Bình Thạnh gave its ward by number. The map shows those 20 numbered wards, with the 5 wards of the redraw drawn over them. The new wards carry names instead: Gia Định, Bình Thạnh, Bình Lợi Trung, Thạnh Mỹ Tây and Bình Quới.

Several of those names are old ones. Bình Lợi Trung is written on the French survey of the creeks, and Bình Quới there as part of the village name Bình Quới Tây; Gia Định and Thạnh Mỹ Tây are printed on the American sheet. Both maps are earlier in this chapter. How the names returned is told in the sister site's story The old names came back.

Which numbered ward did you know? The sister site's The Road Beneath the Road lays five old maps over the same district, to fade in and out yourself.

Never Far from a Chain

Try to walk somewhere a chain store can't reach. You will not get far.

A chain store is never a long walk away. We found 177 outlets belonging to 42 brands: convenience stores, coffee chains, bank branches. The busiest brand alone runs 24 of them.

94%of the district's walkable streets lie within a one-kilometre walk of a chain outlet

Then we walked the street network outward from every outlet. Of 469.3 km of walkable streets, only 28.3 km lie more than a kilometre's walk from the nearest one, shown in dark ink on the map.

Reach is not the same as presence. A street inside the reach may have no chain on it at all; the map only says one is within a kilometre's walk. Where do the chains stop and the independents begin? → The Coffee District, p. 01

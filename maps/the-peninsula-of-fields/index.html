The open peninsula

On the 1984 sheet the peninsula has houses, tracks and a creek, but none of the built-up fill the sheet prints red and the map traces over as a solid fill.

Thanh Đa is the loop of land the Saigon River wraps around, closed off by the Thanh Đa canal. On the 1984 sheet it carries none of the red built-up fill that the map traces over as a solid fill. The 0.2% that the colour reading finds is speckle along the edges of drawn roads.

0.2%of the peninsula's land on this sheet reads as the sheet's red: the few solid specks on the map, speckle along drawn road edges, not built-up fill

What the sheet draws instead is single houses strung along the road to the ferry, dashed tracks, and a creek it spells Rach Ong Ngu, without tone marks. Even the estate built at the peninsula's neck in the 1960s appears as rows of separate buildings, not as red fill.

The sheet stops short of the peninsula's eastern end. It holds 81.4% of Phường Bình Quới, the ward that covers the peninsula, and the share is of that part.

278 buildings. No listed kitchens.

One ordinary block of hẻm, every building drawn, as the online food map sees it.

This block was picked by a rule, not by eye. We cut the district's core into squares, kept the ones with at least as many buildings as a typical square and at least four in five of them set back from the avenues, and took the one with the fewest listings for its size. We then read each of its listings by hand: a house-for-rent advert and a shop selling dried pomelo peel. Neither is a kitchen.

278buildings in this one block, each drawn in grey; a building holding a listed kitchen would turn red

A listing exists only where someone made a page for it online. Whether this block holds kitchens that never did, we cannot say from the data.

So how many kitchens does this block really hold? This data cannot answer that, but a walk down its hẻm can.

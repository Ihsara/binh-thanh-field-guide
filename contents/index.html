Where Bình Thạnh Eats

  1. 01 The Coffee District

    Coffee and milk tea fill a remarkable share of this district's food map.

  2. 02 A Dead Heat

    Cafés or restaurants? It depends on who is counting.

  3. 03 The Bún Belt

    Go ward by ward, and in almost every one, the dish on the most signs is bún.

  4. 04 Little Tokyo on the Canal

    Where Bình Thạnh meets the canal, its Japanese kitchens crowd into a few blocks.

  5. 05 Never Far from a Chain

    Try to walk somewhere a chain store can't reach. You will not get far.

  6. 06 What the Map Can't See

    The free map of the world lists 62 cafés here. A commercial map lists 2,126.

What It Used to Be

  1. 07 Where the City Stopped

    On the 1984 map, the built-up city, printed red and traced here in teal, covers only part of Bình Thạnh.

  2. 08 A District of Creeks

    Before the city reached it, this was villages, trees and open ground, cut through by creeks.

  3. 09 The Road Before the Roads

    Historians trace the old Mandarin Road along two of today's streets.

  4. 10 Before the Towers

    Before the tower that became the country's tallest, this riverbank was a military port.

  5. 11 The Open Peninsula

    On the 1984 sheet the peninsula has houses, tracks and a creek, but none of the built-up fill the sheet prints red and the map traces in teal.

  6. 12 Twenty Wards, Then Five

    Until 2025 the district had 20 numbered wards; the same ground is now 5 named ones.

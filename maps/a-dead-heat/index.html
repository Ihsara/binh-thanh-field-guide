A Dead Heat

Cafés or restaurants? It depends on who is counting.

It depends on who is counting. Overture's labels give 1,230 coffee shops and 1,218 restaurants, close enough to call a draw.

1,230places labelled “coffee shop” — against 1,218 labelled “restaurant”

A label is a decision someone made about a shop. Overture gives each place one main label, so a shop that sells more than one thing still has to land in one box.

The two maps show where each label gathers. Both crowd into Gia Định and Thạnh Mỹ Tây and thin out in Bình Quới, though their densest blocks are not always the same ones. Count the way the volunteers of the free map do and you get a different answer. → What the Map Can't See, p. 06

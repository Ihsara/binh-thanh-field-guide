Where Bình Thạnh Eats

  1. 01 Bình Thạnh eats in pockets

    Its food is not spread evenly: one kind of kitchen keeps crowding into a few streets.

  2. 02 Little Tokyo on Phạm Viết Chánh

    A few blocks from the Cầu Thị Nghè 2 bridge, places listed as Japanese crowd together.

  3. 03 Three campuses, two pockets

    Places listed as Korean gather between two university campuses, and places listed as cafés beside a third.

  4. 04 Little Seoul between two campuses

    A small circle of streets near UTH and HUTECH, where kimbap and mì cay turn up in the names of places listed as Korean.

  5. 05 Online, Bình Thạnh eats on its avenues

    Most of its buildings stand back in the hẻm; most of its listed kitchens face the avenues.

  6. 06 278 buildings. No listed kitchens.

    One ordinary block of hẻm, every building drawn, as the online food map sees it.

What It Used to Be

  1. 07 Where the city stopped

    On the 1984 map, the built-up city, printed red on the sheet and traced over it here as a solid fill, covers only part of Bình Thạnh.

  2. 08 A district of creeks

    Before the city reached it, this was villages, trees and open ground, cut through by creeks.

  3. 09 The road before the roads

    Historians trace the old Mandarin Road along two of today's streets.

  4. 10 Before the towers

    Before the tower that became the country's tallest, this riverbank was a military port.

  5. 11 The open peninsula

    On the 1984 sheet the peninsula has houses, tracks and a creek, but none of the built-up fill the sheet prints red and the map traces over as a solid fill.

  6. 12 Twenty wards, then five

    Until 2025 the district had 20 numbered wards; the same ground is now 5 named ones.

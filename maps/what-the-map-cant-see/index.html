What the Map Can't See

The free map of the world lists 62 cafés here. A commercial map lists 2,126.

2.9%as many cafés on OpenStreetMap as Overture Maps lists

Every map in this chapter rests on a choice of map. OpenStreetMap, built by volunteers, listed 62 cafés in Bình Thạnh in its latest year, against 77 restaurants. Overture Maps, built from company and web data, counts 2,126 café-like places. Each square here is one listing.

Neither is the truth. The free map holds only what its volunteers have added; the commercial one is built from listings, and a listing can outlive its shop. And both miss the cart that parks at the same corner every morning, the woman with a basket of bánh bò, the drinks stall that folds into a doorway at night.

35 listings with a house number name Hẻm 47/52 Bùi Đình Túy. 13 of them, from 12 different doors, give a house number from another alley. Read the way How we know sets out, a house number with no slash is a house in the hẻm the listing names, read as that alley's own. A number with slashes is in the alley given by its numbers before the last slash. A door that gives only the alley's last number is read as the alley's own and is not counted.

Doors are counted, not listings: listings that share a first address line count as one door, so many listings at one door count once.

The map draws every alley OpenStreetMap names on its street where a listed door gives another alley's number. The thicker lines are the alleys where two or more different doors do it. Why the numbers disagree, the listings do not say. How we know gives the rule, and the alleys OpenStreetMap could not match.

The doors, as the listings give them: 122/56a, 125/57E, 183/4, 214/12, 217/20, 229/3, 24/31, 304/48, 304/55B, 47/24/2A, 47/24/38AB, 47/24/7.

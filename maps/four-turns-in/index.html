Of the 123 place-of-worship listings in Bình Thạnh with a house number, 61 give an alley address. Of all 24,349 numbered listings in the district, 30% do.

123 place-of-worship listings in Bình Thạnh give a house number, and 61 of them give an alley address: a number with at least one slash. We read each slash as one more alley in from the street the address names. For shops, offices, schools, food and every other kind of listing, the share is about 3 in 10.

Each coloured line on the map is a route from the street the address names, along the streets and alleys OpenStreetMap draws, to the point Overture lists. Where OpenStreetMap has no match for the street an address names, the route starts on the nearest main road instead; How we know counts them. The three in the second colour are the ones the notes name: two temples listed three alleys in, and the longest drawn way in, on the Thanh Đa peninsula.

A few routes are not drawn. How we know names each of them and why, and gives the full address of every place the notes name.

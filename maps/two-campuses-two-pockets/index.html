Two campuses, two pockets

Two of the district's food pockets sit at university gates.

Two of Bình Thạnh's food pockets sit at university gates. Places labelled Korean gather on Nguyễn Gia Trí, outside HUTECH, a technology university: the pocket's centre is 93 m from the campus. Cafés crowd Đặng Thùy Trâm, 83 m from Văn Lang University. The outlined shapes on the map are the campuses.

93 mfrom the centre of Little Seoul to HUTECH's campus; the café street is 83 m from Văn Lang University

The pockets are not equally sharp. At HUTECH, places labelled Korean are 5.7× as common as across the district. On Đặng Thùy Trâm, cafés are 2.1× as common, 38 of them in one small circle of streets. Cafés are everywhere in Bình Thạnh, so even a street of them can only stand out so far.

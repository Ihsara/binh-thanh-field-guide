Bình Thạnh eats in pockets

Its food is not spread evenly: one kind of kitchen keeps crowding into a few streets.

Bình Thạnh does not eat evenly; it eats in pockets. We moved a small circle across the map, keeping each spot where one kind of kitchen is at least twice as common as across the district. Each pocket's streets take its colour; grey stretches around them carry the other places listed as food.

7pockets on this map: a few streets each, where one kind of kitchen is at least twice as common as across the district

Most sit next to something you can find on a map. Little Tokyo lies a few blocks from Cầu Thị Nghè 2. Little Seoul sits between two university campuses, UTH and HUTECH; the café street in the hẻm off Đặng Thùy Trâm sits by Văn Lang University. One seafood and nhậu pocket wraps the Hàng Xanh roundabout on Xô Viết Nghệ Tĩnh; the other sits just off the Phạm Văn Đồng boulevard. Both quán chay pockets hide in the hẻm off Phan Đăng Lưu and off Lê Quang Định; what draws them there, the map does not say.

Three campuses, two pockets

Places listed as Korean gather between two university campuses, and places listed as cafés beside a third.

Two of Bình Thạnh's food pockets sit beside university campuses. Places listed as Korean gather on Nguyễn Gia Trí and Võ Oanh, between two of them: UTH is across the road, and HUTECH, a technology university, is 8 minutes' walk away. Places listed as cafés gather around Đặng Thùy Trâm, 2 minutes' walk from Văn Lang University. The outlined shapes on the map are the campuses; the minutes are walked along the streets, not measured as the crow flies.

6.6×as common in Little Seoul as across the district, for places listed as Korean; on the café street, places listed as cafés are 2× as common

The pockets are not equally sharp. In Little Seoul, places listed as Korean are 6.6× as common as across the district. Around Đặng Thùy Trâm, places listed as cafés are 2× as common, 35 of them in one small circle of streets. Cafés are everywhere in Bình Thạnh, so even a street of them can only stand out so far.

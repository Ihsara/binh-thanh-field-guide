The Coffee District

Coffee and milk tea fill a remarkable share of this district's food map.

Order a coffee here and you have plenty of company. Of the 5,583 places to eat or drink that Overture Maps lists in Bình Thạnh, 2,210 sell coffee or milk tea first. The coloured dots on this map are those coffee places; the grey ones are everything else.

39.6%of every place to eat or drink is a café or a milk-tea shop

Follow the coloured dots and they line up along the streets, thickest in Gia Định and Thạnh Mỹ Tây and sparse in the Bình Quới lobe to the east. The list does not record how big a place is: a one-room café and a chain branch each count as one dot.

So which comes first, the coffee or the restaurant? That depends on who is counting. → A Dead Heat, p. 02

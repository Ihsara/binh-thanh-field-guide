Where the city stopped

On the 1984 map, the built-up city, printed red on the sheet and traced over it here as a solid fill, covers only part of Bình Thạnh.

On the US Defense Mapping Agency sheet of 1984, built-up ground is printed red; the map here traces over that red with a solid fill of its own. Set under the district's outline, the fill covers its west and south: the old town around Bà Chiểu and Gia Định, down to the Thị Nghè creek. The northern lobe, the riverbank where the towers stand now and the Thanh Đa peninsula all lie outside it.

17.6%of the district's land on this sheet lies inside the built-up fill, printed red on the sheet and traced over it on the map as a solid fill

The sheet ends before the district does. It holds 94.3% of the district, the eastern tip runs off its edge, and the share is of the part on the sheet. The fill is read by its red colour, and every colour setting that reads our hand-checked test squares equally well puts the share between 15.1% and 19.1%. Take the big number as a rough share, not an exact one.
